# Public work relevant to Exynos 5300 / Pixel 8 Pro

Checked 2026-11-14. Research used Exa across three angles: direct PC5/sidelink demonstrations, diagnostic/firmware access, and independent Linux modem control. Five search calls requested 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 6 = 38 result entries, including duplicates. Primary project documentation and first-person accounts were fetched for validation; search-result counts are not counts of independently validated projects.

## Finding

There is useful public work on this modem family: modem diagnostics, configuration changes, firmware analysis and custom host-side modem control. No reproducible direct phone-to-phone cellular PC5/ProSe/sidelink demonstration on an Exynos 5300 Pixel was found in the reviewed results. This is a search finding, not proof that such support is impossible or absent from all firmware.

The strongest actual 5300 control demonstration found is Steffen Deusch's first-person Pixel 9a work. It establishes conventional modem control, not tower-free direct communication. The Pixel 9a and Pixel 7/7 Pro evidence must not be presented as a test of this exact Pixel 8 Pro and its Android 17 firmware.

## Implication for our app

Custom Linux control, diagnostics and configuration are feasible research directions. They do not establish an offline cellular transport for the app. The earlier Pixel 8 Pro status/version probe only established local CPIF driver access. Our Android-side string/service searches were limited and cannot exclude features inside modem firmware.

The most relevant further investigation would inspect the phone's matching firmware and SIT message catalogue for PC5, ProSe, sidelink or V2X support, then identify a documented query or configuration path and a compatible second peer. Discovering a string or parser alone would still not establish a working over-the-air link.

No additional commands were sent to the modem and no device settings were changed during this web research.
